Publications and research outputs

Research on Chinese foreign policy, international order, and the Middle East.

My publications examine economic diplomacy, China’s relations with Middle Eastern actors, international image and strategic narratives, and leadership perceptions in policy formation. The articles below were published in Persian; English translations of their titles are provided for international readers.

International Security is a Persian-language policy periodical published by the Tehran International Studies & Research Institute (TISRI). It should not be confused with the English-language journal of the same name published by MIT Press.

Thesis

ادراکات شی جین‌پینگ و ابتکار کمربند و راه

Xi Jinping’s Perceptions and the Belt and Road Initiative

Mofidi Chelan, M. (2026). Xi Jinping’s perceptions and the Belt and Road Initiative [M.A. thesis, University of Tehran].

Read summary

The thesis reconstructs Xi Jinping’s causal beliefs to examine how leadership interpretation shaped the policy logic and political integration of the Belt and Road Initiative. It uses qualitative analysis and cognitive mapping to connect perceptions of international order, development, national rejuvenation, and political legitimacy.

Policy research articles

A Critical Assessment of the Chinese Narrative of International Order

Mofidi Chelan, M. (2024). A critical assessment of the Chinese narrative of international order. The TISRI Narrative, 1(1), 255–281.

Read summary

This article evaluates the vision of global governance advanced in China’s narrative of a ‘global community of shared future.’ It examines the narrative’s diagnosis of international disorder, its normative alternatives, and the gap between broad principles and practical implementation.
