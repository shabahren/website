Projects

Research developed across academic, policy, and public-facing projects.

These projects trace the development of my research from China-focused policy studies to the analysis of leadership perceptions, strategic narratives, and narrative reception.

Current research

Iranian and Chinese Strategic Narratives of International Order

Research in progress · 2026–present

This project compares how Iran and China explain the origins of international disorder and the pathways through which peace, security, and development can be achieved. It distinguishes shared normative language—such as sovereignty, multilateralism, and institutional reform—from the causal logic connecting problems, solutions, and desired outcomes in each country’s official narrative.

The research uses qualitative coding and cognitive mapping of speeches delivered at the United Nations General Assembly. Preliminary analysis contrasts Chinese emphasis on development, modernization, connectivity, and multilateral governance with Iranian emphasis on sanctions, coercion, intervention, sovereignty, justice, and sources of disorder.

Narrative Reception and Foreign-Policy Interaction in the Middle East

Developing research agenda · China–Middle East relations

This research asks how political actors in the Middle East interpret, selectively adopt, resist, or strategically appropriate Chinese narratives, and when rhetorical alignment reflects deeper changes in causal beliefs. It connects narrative reception to foreign-policy interaction without reducing policy change either to discourse alone or to material incentives alone.

Completed research

Xi Jinping’s Perceptions and the Belt and Road Initiative

M.A. thesis · University of Tehran · 2023–2026

This project examined how Xi Jinping’s perceptions contributed to the formation of the Belt and Road Initiative. It used qualitative content analysis and cognitive mapping to reconstruct causal beliefs expressed in selected speeches and interviews, connecting leadership perceptions to international and domestic problems, development, national rejuvenation, and the political integration of inherited policy instruments.

The thesis received a grade of Excellent.

China’s Rise: Dimensions and Implications for Iran

Institutional research project · Center for Chinese Research, Allameh Tabataba’i University · 2022–2023

This project examined different dimensions of China’s rise and their implications for Iran. My contribution included research support, coordination with contributors, editorial review, citation checking, cross-chapter consistency, interview transcription and thematic analysis, and preparation of research materials for publication and dissemination.

Exploring China’s Negotiating Culture for Middle Managers

Commissioned policy research · Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Iran · 2022

This report documented elite experiences and examined features of Chinese negotiating culture relevant to Iranian managers and officials. The work combined document-based research with the organization and interpretation of practitioner knowledge. The report is not publicly available.
